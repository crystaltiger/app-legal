Privacy Policy

SnoreLog (安睡宝) for Android · Effective November 10, 2026
In one sentence: SnoreLog records and analyzes audio entirely on your phone. It has no Internet permission and no networking code — your data cannot leave the device.

1. Data we process

  • Microphone audio — recorded only during a session you start, stored in the App's private folder; short clips are kept for replay only if "Save snore clips" is on.
  • Statistics and settings — event counts, durations, level grades and your preferences, stored in a local database on your phone.

The bundled YAMNet classifier (3.9 MB) runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

2. What we never do

  • No collection, transmission, sale or sharing of any personal data
  • No account, sign-in or identifiers (no device or advertising ID)
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No access to contacts, location, camera or other apps' data

3. Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO — night recording; you grant it at first start and can revoke it anytime
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE + WAKE_LOCK — keeps recording running with the screen off
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — the required ongoing "Recording" notification (Android 13+)

4. Deleting your data

  • Single night: the delete button in History removes that night's events, stats and clips
  • Audio only: Settings → "Saved clips on disk" → "Delete all"
  • Everything: uninstall the App — Android removes all local data automatically

There is no server copy, so nothing needs deleting on our side.

5. Children

SnoreLog is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone — child or adult — because it cannot transmit data.

6. Health note

SnoreLog is a recording tool, not a medical device. Breath pauses or daytime sleepiness can be signs of sleep apnea — please consult a doctor.
